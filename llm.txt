# Webnool

> Webnool designs and builds fast, single-page websites and landing pages for small businesses across Kerala — engineered for speed, SEO, and WhatsApp/Instagram integration.

## About
- Webnool is a website studio based in Kerala, India, building landing pages and single-page websites for small and local businesses.
- Tagline: "Design. Develop. Grow."
- Process: Discover, Design, Build, Launch, Grow. See [Process](https://webnool.com/#process).

## Services
- [Landing Pages](https://webnool.com/#services): High-converting pages built around a single goal — a booking, a call, or a sale.
- [Single-Page Websites](https://webnool.com/#services): A full business site — about, services, gallery, contact — in one scroll.
- [SEO Optimization](https://webnool.com/#services): Clean headings, structured data, and search-ready copy.
- [Page Speed](https://webnool.com/#speed): Lean, hand-built pages targeting sub-1s load times.
- [Social Media Integration](https://webnool.com/#social): WhatsApp click-to-chat and an Instagram follow button, built into every page.
- [Care & Updates](https://webnool.com/#services): Ongoing content changes and small fixes after launch.
